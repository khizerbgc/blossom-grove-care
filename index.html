Our Homes

Welcome to Willow Grove House

Willow Grove House has been thoughtfully designed to provide a calm, comfortable and nurturing environment where children can feel at ease. Every space has been carefully considered to create the warmth and familiarity of a family home, while providing the stability and support children need to flourish.

From shared spaces that encourage connection to private bedrooms that offer comfort and security, our home is designed to help children feel a genuine sense of belonging from the moment they walk through the door.

A Warm, Family Home

Homely interiors and shared spaces designed to feel comfortable, not clinical.

Calm Spaces

Quiet, considered environments that help children feel settled and secure.

Small by Design

A small number of children in every home, so each one is truly known.

Our Approach

Relationships are at the heart of everything we do

We believe positive relationships create the foundation for growth. By providing consistency, understanding and genuine care, we create an environment where children feel safe to build trust, develop confidence and take positive steps forward.

Every child is different, which is why we take the time to understand their individual needs, strengths and aspirations. Working alongside families, social workers, education providers and healthcare professionals, we provide care that is thoughtful, consistent and centred around each child's journey.

Referrals

Making a referral

We understand that finding the right home is an important decision. Our team aims to make the referral process straightforward, transparent and collaborative, working closely with placing authorities to determine whether Willow Grove House is the right fit for a child.

If you would like to discuss a referral, we're always happy to have an initial conversation.

1

Initial Enquiry

Get in touch to discuss the child's needs and ask any questions about our home.

2

Assessment

We'll carefully review the referral information and work with you to determine whether we can meet the child's needs.

3

Decision & Planning

If the placement is appropriate, we'll work together to plan a smooth and well-supported transition into the home.
